Miku Fan Club Official Website

Welcome to the ultimate fan club for virtual pop singer (or vocaloid for short) Hatsune Miku. This is the place to chat, share, and find like minded people who are into vocaloid as you. It does not matter if you are a casual or hardcore fan, everybody is welcome at the Hatsune Miku fan club.

Perk of being a member

Members get access to chatroom where fan can meet and talk to likeminded individuals!
We host meetups at cons to allow members to take pictures and find new friends!
Artists and vocaloid producer are free to advertise themselves
We host community events either online or sometime in person!
